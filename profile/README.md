We assess repetitive, document-intensive workflows and build a suitable solution when the value
justifies the effort. Most engagements start with a focused feasibility assessment, so the decision
to continue rests on evidence rather than on a proposal.

### What we do

- **AI feasibility assessment** — where AI is useful, where simpler automation is enough, and what
  should stay with your team
- **Software development** — applications for document-intensive workflows, ERP environments and
  regulated domains
- **Technical AI governance and security** — GDPR, EU AI Act and information-security requirements
  translated into technical and organisational controls
- **AI evaluation and monitoring** — response quality tested against your own cases, regressions
  caught, production failures made visible

### Proxytrace

Proxytrace is the software we develop in-house. It records model requests and responses, checks
results against your test cases, and tracks quality and operational behaviour over time. Deployable
on your own infrastructure.
